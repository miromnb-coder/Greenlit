# Greenlit

Nothing reaches a customer until you greenlight it.

Greenlit is an inbound lead engine for small B2B service companies. A new website lead is researched, drafted, and queued. A human approves the message. Then Greenlit sends it, logs it to the CRM, and follows the thread to a meeting or a clear no.

This is not a chatbot. It is not an autonomous sales department. It is a controlled lane from form submit to first conversation.

## Promise

> When a lead comes in, Greenlit researches it, writes the first email, and gets it ready to send. You hit approve. The lead does not sit in the inbox.

## Who it is for

B2B service firms, 5–40 people, selling through a website form + email: consultancies, agencies, specialist SaaS, training, professional services.

## What v1 does

1. Lead in (webhook, form, CSV, HubSpot)
2. Research (company, role, likely need, score)
3. Draft (first email + internal reason)
4. Approval queue (edit / approve / reject)
5. Send from the customer’s Gmail or Outlook
6. Write the CRM note
7. When they reply: classify and draft the next step

Human approval is required for every outbound message in v1.

## What v1 does not do

- Cold outbound
- Phone agents
- “Tell us a goal, we invent any workflow”
- An AI CEO that reads the whole company
- A custom model

Those are later layers. They are earned after inbound works.
