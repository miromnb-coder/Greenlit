# Customer interview script

Goal: 10 conversations in 7 days with owners or sales leads at 5–40 person B2B service firms.

## Who to call

- Consultancy, agency, specialist SaaS, training, professional services
- They already get website form leads
- They sell over email, not only inbound chat
- Finland / Nordics first, English-ready firms also fine

## Open

“I am building a tool that handles new website leads: research, first email, follow-up, CRM. You approve every send. Can I ask how you do this today? Fifteen minutes.”

## Questions

1. How many new website leads do you get in a week?
2. Who touches a new lead first, and after how many hours?
3. What happens when that person is in a meeting or on holiday?
4. Show me (or describe) the last lead that went cold. Where did it die?
5. What must never be said in the first email?
6. What would make a first email good enough to send without rewriting?
7. Which CRM do you actually open every day?
8. Would you let software send as you if you approved the text first?
9. If this ran 20 of your real leads next week, what would you measure?
10. What would you pay per month if time-to-first-email dropped from hours to 15 minutes?

## Close

“If I stood this up for your next 20 leads, would you try it? What would block that?”

## Score after the call

- Lead volume: low / enough / high
- Pain: mild / sharp / urgent
- Approval comfort: no / maybe / yes
- CRM: none / messy / HubSpot or Pipedrive
- Trial: no / conditional / yes

Build if you have three “yes” or “conditional” trials. Rewrite the offer if you have zero.
